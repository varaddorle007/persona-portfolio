I am a driven Full-Stack Architect & AI Engineer with a relentless focus on native mobile, cross-device systems, AI models, and custom hardware engineering.


I thrive at the intersection of hardware and software. My philosophy is simple: build useful tools, understand systems down to the metal, experiment aggressively, and ship resilient software that performs with zero fluff.


My core competencies span Android native (Kotlin & Jetpack Compose), real-time WebSockets, deep learning (PyTorch & transformers), distributed backends (Node.js & FastAPI), and embedded hardware (ZMK, ESP32, KiCad).


One of my flagship hardware projects is deck-8, an open-source 8-key macro pad with dual rotary knobs, OLED display, custom multi-layer PCB, and ZMK Bluetooth firmware for cross-device desktop productivity.


In the mobile & systems space, I engineered StreamLink Deck, a high-performance control surface bridging native Android and Node.js PC host over low-latency bi-directional sockets for OBS and macro automation.


In machine learning, I created Levo AI, a GPT-style autoregressive transformer language model designed and trained entirely from scratch in PyTorch with custom tokenization and causal attention.


I also engineered CortexCAD Compute for volumetric geometry processing with CT scans and PicoGK, and WristCursor, a Wear OS gesture air mouse that turns smartwatch telemetry into PC cursor motion.


Core technologies I deploy daily include Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Python, PyTorch, TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js, FastAPI, C/C++, Docker, Linux, Git, WebSockets, KiCad, and ADB.


I value speed, deep technical curiosity, and end-to-end execution. Whether it is etching PCB traces, optimizing tensor graphs, or architecting low-latency sockets, I love tackling complex engineering challenges from first principles.
